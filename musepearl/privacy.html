Privacy Policy · MusePearl

Last updated: 2026-10-05 ｜ Effective: on release of the applicable version

Short version: MusePearl is a local-first inspiration library. In the current version, your inspiration content and selected media are stored on your device by default. We do not operate an account system or a MusePearl content server.

1. Scope

This policy explains how MusePearl handles information when you use the current iOS or Android version. It applies to the app, its local data features, support contact, system sharing, and in-app purchase flows.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Inspiration text, tags, status, favorites, dates, supplements, and other content you create.
  • Images, thumbnails, and attachments that you choose or create in the app.
  • Local preferences such as appearance, language, content display, and other app settings.
  • Local membership entitlement state needed to provide purchased features.

This information is stored locally using the platform and app storage mechanisms. The current version does not provide MusePearl account login, a MusePearl-operated content server, or iCloud / CloudKit sync.

3. Photos, camera, and notifications

  • Photo library access is used only when you choose to select or save media through the app.
  • Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo through the app.
  • Notification access, when offered and authorized, is used only for the notification feature you enable.

You can deny or later revoke these permissions in system settings. The app may provide a reduced experience when a permission is unavailable.

4. Sharing, export, and external services

When you explicitly use system sharing, copying, printing, exporting, email feedback, or another external action, the information you choose is passed to the system or destination app you select. That destination's own privacy policy applies to its subsequent handling.

Feedback emails are sent through your chosen mail service to g365805967@gmail.com. We receive the contents and sender information included in that email.

5. Membership and purchases

Membership purchases and subscription management are processed by the applicable app store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. MusePearl does not receive your full payment card details. The app may receive transaction, purchase, renewal, restoration, and entitlement status needed to enable the features associated with your purchase. The app store's privacy policy and terms also apply.

6. Collection, advertising, and tracking

The current version does not include a MusePearl account service, advertising network, or analytics service operated by us for the purpose of building an advertising profile. We do not sell your inspiration content. This statement does not override information handled by Apple, Google, your email provider, your selected sharing destination, or other third-party services you choose to use.

7. Retention and deletion

Your local content remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data where supported, or uninstall the app. Because the current version does not maintain a MusePearl content server, we do not retain a server-side copy of your local inspiration library.

8. Children

MusePearl is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request or collect children's personal information through a MusePearl-operated account or server.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: g365805967@gmail.com

If a translated version conflicts with the English version, the English version controls to the extent permitted by applicable law.

隐私政策 · 灵珠 MusePearl

更新日期：2026-10-05 ｜ 生效日期：适用版本发布之日

简要说明：灵珠是一款本地优先的灵感库。当前版本中，你创建的灵感内容和选择的媒体默认保存在你的设备上。我们不提供灵珠账号体系，也没有由我们运营的灵珠内容服务器。

1. 适用范围

本政策说明你使用当前 iOS 或 Android 版本灵珠时的信息处理方式，涵盖本地数据功能、支持联系、系统分享和应用内购买流程。

2. 保存在设备上的信息

  • 你创建的灵感正文、标签、状态、收藏、日期、补充内容及其他内容。
  • 你在应用中选择或创建的图片、缩略图和附件。
  • 外观、语言、内容显示和其他应用设置等本地偏好。
  • 用于提供已购买功能的本地会员权益状态。

这些信息使用平台和应用的本地存储机制保存。当前版本不提供灵珠账号登录、灵珠自营内容服务器，也不提供 iCloud / CloudKit 同步。

3. 相册、相机和通知

  • 相册权限仅在你主动选择或保存媒体时使用。
  • 相机权限仅在你主动通过应用拍摄照片时使用。
  • 当应用提供通知功能且你授权后，通知权限仅用于你开启的通知功能。

你可以拒绝权限，也可以在系统设置中之后撤回。权限不可用时，应用可能提供受限体验。

4. 分享、导出和外部服务

当你主动使用系统分享、复制、打印、导出、反馈邮件或其他外部操作时，你选择的信息会交给系统或你选择的目标应用。后续如何处理这些信息，以目标服务自身的隐私政策为准。

反馈邮件会通过你选择的邮件服务发送至 g365805967@gmail.com。我们会收到邮件中包含的内容和发件人信息。

5. 会员和购买

会员购买和订阅管理由适用的应用商店处理，例如 Apple App Store 或 Google Play。灵珠不会收到你的完整银行卡信息。应用可能接收启用已购买功能所需的交易、购买、续订、恢复和权益状态。应用商店自身的隐私政策和条款也适用。

6. 收集、广告和追踪

当前版本不包含由我们运营的灵珠账号服务、广告网络，也不提供用于建立广告画像的分析服务。我们不会出售你的灵感内容。本条不覆盖 Apple、Google、邮件服务商、你选择的分享目标或其他由你主动使用的第三方服务所处理的信息。

7. 保存期限和删除

你的本地内容会保存在设备上，直到你在应用中删除、在支持的平台上清除应用数据，或卸载应用。由于当前版本没有灵珠内容服务器，我们不会在服务器端保留你的本地灵感库副本。

8. 儿童

灵珠不面向儿童。我们不会通过灵珠运营的账号或服务器明知地要求或收集儿童个人信息。

9. 政策变更

当应用的数据处理方式或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新后的政策会发布在本页面，并更新“更新日期”；在适当情况下，重大变化也可能在应用内提示。
