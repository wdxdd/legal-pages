Privacy Policy · Oh Keys

Last updated: 2026-09-27 ｜ Effective: on release

1. Overview

"Oh Keys" runs locally on your device to check the balance and usage of third-party LLM API keys that you enter yourself. The App has no account system and no server operated by us; we do not collect, upload, or share your personal information.

2. How we handle your data

  • API keys and card info are stored only on your device (encrypted via iOS Keychain / Android Keystore / platform Keystore).
  • Balance / usage queries are sent directly from your device to the provider endpoint you configured, carrying your API key for authentication. We do not proxy, relay, or log these requests. The provider's handling is governed by their own privacy policy.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. The App contains no ads and no analytics SDKs.

3. Storage and retention

Your data stays on your device; uninstalling the App removes it. We run no server, so there is no server-side retention.

4. Network and background

The App uses the network only for queries you start, or for the periodic refresh you enable (minimum interval ≈ 60 minutes). It is never used for advertising or tracking.

5. Permissions

  • Network: for your balance / usage queries.
  • iOS: no Face ID, location, microphone, or photo permissions are used.
  • Android: an optional background task may be used for periodic refresh; you can disable it in system settings.

6. Children

The App targets adult developers and users, not children, and does not collect children's data.

7. Your control

You can delete cards/keys, disable auto-refresh, or uninstall the App at any time.

8. Your rights under local law

We honor the access, correction, deletion, and objection rights described in this policy for users everywhere, including rights under South Korea's PIPA, Japan's APPI, Brazil's LGPD, Turkey's KVKK, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, India's DPDP Act, Singapore's and Thailand's PDPA, Vietnam's PDPD, Indonesia's PDP Law and Australia's Privacy Act.
